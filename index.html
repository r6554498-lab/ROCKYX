📄 Legal & Policies

These are starter frontend policy pages. Final legal policies should be reviewed before RockyX launches publicly.

Privacy Policy

RockyX should explain what information is collected, why it is used, how it is protected, retention periods, user choices, location handling, payments, cookies and third-party services.

Terms of Service

Users must follow applicable laws, protect their accounts, provide accurate information and follow RockyX rules. Marketplace, service, creator and payment features may have additional terms.

Cookie Policy

RockyX may use necessary technologies for authentication, security, preferences and platform operation. Optional analytics or advertising technologies should be disclosed.

Community Guidelines

RockyX should not be used for threats, harassment, fraud, impersonation, exploitation, illegal activity, scams, non-consensual intimate content or intellectual-property violations.

Payments & Refunds

Payment, cancellation, refund, seller payout and service booking rules should be clearly displayed before transactions. Final payment rules will be connected during the backend phase.
